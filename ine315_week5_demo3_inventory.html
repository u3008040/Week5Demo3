    Lead-time demand is about 3 × 20 = 60.
    Which values of s are clearly too small? Drag s slowly upward from 20.
    
    
    
    Check your answer
      
    Anything below about 60 stocks out. Exactly 60 is marginal — it covers average
      demand during the wait, so any above-average week still fails. The gap between 60 and your working
      value is what an inventory course calls safety stock.

    It fails again, for the same reason with different numbers: 6 days of waiting needs about 120
